PART 1: THE CORE DEAL ENGINE & USER JOURNEY
Ye poora process Telegram Group ke andar sirf Inline Buttons ke through chalega. UI ekdam minimal, fast aur clean hoga.

Step 1: The Deal Creation (Smart Onboarding)
Jab koi group mein /escrow type karega, toh bot chat mein ek inline menu bhejega (Sirf command dene wale ko dikhega ya group mein clean flow banayega). Bot step-by-step 5 questions puchega:

Role & Product: What do you want to do? [Buy] | [Sell] | [Crypto <-> INR]

Amount: Enter the deal amount. (User types the number, e.g., 5000).

Fee Split: Who pays the 1% Bot Fee? [Buyer] | [Seller] | [Split 50/50]

TOC (Terms & Conditions): Enter specific terms for this deal (e.g., 1-month warranty). User text type kar sakta hai ya [Skip] daba sakta hai.

Tag Counterparty: Mention the user you are dealing with (@username).

Step 2: The "Live-Updating" Deal Card
Saare steps pure hote hi bot group mein ek heavy, professional Live Deal Card bhejega. Ye naye messages spam nahi karega, balki deal ke har stage par yahi ek message edit hota rahega.

Step 3: The Payment Hold (Locking the Funds)
Buyer [Pay Now] par click karega. Bot usko payment method choose karne bolega (External UPI/Crypto ya Inbuilt Vault-Pay).

Jaise hi buyer pay karta hai (aur funds verify hote hain), bot usi group message ko instantly edit karega:

🔵 Status: PAID! Funds are locked in the Vault.
Action Required: Seller, please deliver the product/details to the buyer in DM.

Step 4: The 2-Step Release & Secret Handshake
Seller buyer ko DM mein product de dega. Ab fraud rokne ke liye yahan double-security layer kaam aayegi:

Buyer group mein aakar deal card par [Release Funds ✅] dabayega.

Anti-Fat-Finger Warning: Bot ek pop-up ya edit karke warning dega:
⚠️ Are you 100% sure? Have you checked the product? This action is irreversible.
[Yes, Confirm Release] | [Cancel Action]

The Secret PIN Logic (Optional/Advanced): Deal shuru hote waqt bot ne seller ko ek 4-digit PIN diya tha. Seller ne wo PIN product ke sath buyer ko de diya. Release confirm karte waqt buyer ko wo PIN enter karna hoga. Ye undeniable proof hai ki seller ne delivery ki hai.

Step 5: Auto-Payout & The Glory (Completion)
Jaise hi release confirm hota hai, bot backend se 1% fee kaat kar (jisne bhi fee pay karne ka option chuna tha uske hisaab se) baaki amount Seller ke Inbuilt Wallet ya external UPI/Crypto address par milliseconds mein bhej dega.

Bot group message ko final time edit karega aur usko Auto-Pin kar dega.

Funds successfully transferred to @SellerUser.
[Raise Query 🛑] (Ye button sirf record ya extreme baad wale issues ke liye rahega).

PART 2: THE "VAULT-PAY" FINANCIAL ECOSYSTEM 💰
Is system ki core currency hogi "Vault Credits" (VC). Rule simple hai: 1 VC = 1 INR (ya equal crypto value). Users ko external UPI/Crypto tabhi use karna padega jab unhe apne VC wallet ko top-up (deposit) karna ho ya cash out (withdraw) karna ho.

Step 1: The Wallet Dashboard (/wallet)
Jab user /wallet command dega, bot usko ek personal, highly secure inline dashboard dikhayega:

🏦 YOUR VAULT-PAY DASHBOARD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
👤 User: @BuyerUser
💳 Available Balance: 4,500 VC (₹4,500)
🔒 Locked in Deals: 500 VC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[➕ Deposit Funds] | [➖ Withdraw Funds]
[📜 Transaction History]

Step 2: The Deposit Flow (Manual Admin Review)
Paisa system mein aane ka process 100% secure aur admin-controlled hoga.

User [➕ Deposit Funds] dabayega. Bot amount puchega (e.g., 2000).

Bot puchega: "How do you want to pay?" [UPI (INR)] | [LTC (Crypto)].

Bot tumhara (Admin ka) official payment address dikhayega: "Please pay ₹2000 to admin@upi or send X LTC to address. Then upload the screenshot/TxID here."

User screenshot/TxID upload karega.

The Magic: Bot turant Admin Panel mein ek alert bhejega: "🚨 Deposit Request: @BuyerUser sent a screenshot for 2000 VC."

Tum admin panel se screenshot verify karoge aur [Approve ✅] daba doge. Microseconds mein user ke wallet mein 2000 VC add ho jayenge aur usko notification mil jayega.

Step 3: The Withdrawal Flow (Cash-Out)
System se paisa bahar nikalne ka process bhi strictly tumhare hath mein hoga taaki koi fraud na ho.

User [➖ Withdraw Funds] dabayega. Amount enter karega.

Bot uska external UPI ID ya LTC address maangega.

Jaise hi user confirm karega, bot uske VC balance se turant amount kaat lega (taaki wo unhi paiso se koi aur deal na kar le).

Tumhare Admin Panel mein ping aayega: "💸 Withdrawal Request: @SellerUser wants to withdraw 3000 VC to seller@upi."

Tum aaram se apne bank/crypto app se usko pay karoge, aur bot mein [Mark as Paid ✅] daba doge. User ko confirmation mil jayega.

Step 4: Deal Execution (Zero-Delay Magic)
Jab buyer ke wallet mein VC loaded hain, toh deals ki speed 10x ho jayegi.

Deal card par jab buyer [Pay Now] dabayega, bot puchega: Pay from VC Wallet?

Buyer jaise hi Yes dabayega, bina kisi link par gaye, bina UPI app khole, microseconds mein buyer ke wallet se VC deduct ho jayenge aur "Locked in Deals" mein chale jayenge.

Group ka deal card instantly "PAID" status par update ho jayega. No waiting!

Step 5: The "Fee Math" (Automated Commission)
Tumhara 1% charge automatically background mein calculate hoga deal create hote waqt. Iska math ekdam flawless hoga based on "Fee Split" choice:

Example: ₹10,000 (10,000 VC) ki deal hai. Bot ki fee hai 1% = 100 VC.

Case A (Buyer Pays Fee):
Bot buyer ke wallet se 10,100 VC kaatega. Seller ke wallet mein 10,000 VC daalega. Tumhare Admin Treasury mein 100 VC add ho jayenge.

Case B (Seller Pays Fee):
Bot buyer ke wallet se 10,000 VC kaatega. Seller ke wallet mein 9,900 VC daalega. Tumhare Admin Treasury mein 100 VC add ho jayenge.

Case C (Split 50-50):
Bot buyer ke wallet se 10,050 VC kaatega. Seller ke wallet mein 9,950 VC daalega. Tumhare Admin Treasury mein 100 VC add ho jayenge.
(Ye saara math bot internally karega aur deal final hote hi VC directly wallets mein settle ho jayenge).

Step 6: Refund Routing (Keeping the Money Inside)
Agar koi dispute hota hai ya deal cancel hoti hai, toh buyer ka locked paisa uske bank account mein wapas nahi jayega (usme bohot headache hai).

Refund directly uske Vault-Pay Wallet (VC) mein aayega.

Fayda? User us paise ko withdraw karne ki jagah kisi aur deal mein use karna prefer karega, jisse tumhare ecosystem mein cash flow bana rahega aur volume badhta rahega.

Bhai, is "Vault-Pay" ecosystem ka sabse bada fayda ye hai ki tumhare paas liquid cash rahega, APIs (Razorpay/Stripe) ka koi jhanjhat ya ban hone ka darr nahi hoga, aur transactions ki speed ekdam makhan (smooth) hogi.

Jab tum deposit aur withdraw manual approve karoge, toh poora control hamesha tumhari mutthi mein rahega.

⚖️ Section 2: The Dispute Tribunal (One-Sided Justice Flow)
Jab kisi deal mein lafda hota hai aur user [Raise Query 🛑] dabata hai, tab tumhara headache bachane ke liye ye highly organized flow chalega:

The Silent Upload: Query dabane wale ko bot DM mein bolega: "Upload screenshots/videos of the issue."

The Admin Ping: Tumhare panel mein ek red alert aayega: 🚨 New Ticket: Deal #4092.

The Case File: Tum ticket open karoge. Bot ek message mein us deal ki saari history (Amount, Buyer, Seller, T&C) aur user ke proofs dikhayega.

The Admin Interrogation: Tum panel se ek button dabaoge: [Ping Seller]. Bot automatically seller ko message karega: "Admin has summoned you for Deal #4092. The buyer claims X. Provide your proof here."

The God Judgement (Action Buttons): Dono side dekhne ke baad tumhare paas 3 buttons honge:

[Force Release to Seller ✅] (Agar buyer ne fraud proof diya)

[Force Refund to Buyer 🔄] (Agar seller ne scam kiya)

[Split 50-50 ⚖️] (Agar aam-sehmati se matter settle karna ho)
Jaise hi tum button dabaoge, funds Vault Credits (VC) mein settle ho jayenge aur ticket close.

🏦 Section 3: The Financial Queue (Wallet Operations)
Ye wo section hai jahan se Part 2 ka manual deposit aur withdraw control hoga.

Tumhe ek queue dikhegi: Pending Deposits: 3 | Pending Withdrawals: 2

Approve Deposit: Tum click karoge, bot dikhayega: "User A deposited ₹2000. [Screenshot attached]". Tum screenshot apne bank app se milaoge aur [Approve ✅] daba doge. Uske wallet mein instantly VC add ho jayenge.

Approve Withdrawal: Bot dikhayega: "User B wants to withdraw 3000 VC to UPI ID: xyz@upi". Tum apne app se usko pay karoge aur bot mein [Mark as Paid ✅] daba doge. Uska VC balance permanently deduct ho jayega.

🚨 Section 4: The "Guillotine" (Scammer Control & Security)
Agar koi scammer pakda jata hai, toh use hamesha ke liye system se bahar nikalne ke liye tumhare paas extreme powers hongi:

[Freeze Wallet 🧊]: Agar tumhe kisi user par doubt hai, tum uski Telegram ID daal kar uska VC Wallet freeze kar sakte ho. Wo na naya deal kar payega, na paise withdraw kar payega.

[Global Blacklist 🛑]: Kisi confirmed scammer ka UPI ID, LTC address, ya Telegram ID blacklist kar do. Agar wo naya account banakar wahi payment address use karega, bot automatically deal rok dega.

[Wipe Reputation 🗑️]: Agar kisi ne fake accounts banakar apne aap ko +vouch de diye hain, tum ek click mein uski 5-Star rating aur vouches zero kar sakte ho.

⚙️ Section 5: Dynamic System Controls
Bot ke code ko bina chhue (without touching the backend), tum live system change kar sakte ho:

[Change Bot Fee %]: Tyohar (festival) ya promotion ke liye tum 1% fee ko 0.5% ya 2% mein instantly badal sakte ho.

[Broadcast Message 📢]: Poore ecosystem (saare bot users) ko ek sath message bhejna (e.g., "System will be down for 10 mins for maintenance" ya koi promotion offer).

[Toggle Maintenance Mode 🚧]: Ek click se saari nayi deals banna ruk jayengi, sirf purani deals process hongi jab tak tum isko wapas on na karo.
